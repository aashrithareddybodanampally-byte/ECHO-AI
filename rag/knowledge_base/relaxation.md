# Relaxation Techniques

## Slow breathing
Slow breathing can help when you feel tense or overwhelmed. Try breathing in gently through the nose for about four seconds, then out slowly for about six seconds, and repeat for a few minutes. A longer out-breath than in-breath is often calming.

## Grounding with the senses
When thoughts are racing, grounding can bring attention back to the present. Notice five things you can see, four you can feel, three you can hear, two you can smell and one you can taste.

## Progressive muscle relaxation
Tense one muscle group, such as the hands or shoulders, for a few seconds, then release it and notice the difference. Move slowly through the body from feet to face.

## Taking a short break from stress
Stepping away for a short walk, drinking water, listening to music or talking with a friend can reduce the intensity of stressful moments. Small breaks are not wasted time; they often make the next block of work easier.
