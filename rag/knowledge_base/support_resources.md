# Professional Support Resources

## When to seek professional help
If low mood, anxiety, stress or sleep problems last for weeks, keep getting worse, or make it hard to study, work or look after yourself, it is a good idea to talk to a doctor, counsellor or licensed mental-health professional. Many colleges and universities offer free counselling services.

## Crisis and emergency support
If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services right away. In India, the Tele-MANAS mental health helpline is available at 14416. In the US and Canada, you can call or text 988. For other countries, findahelpline.com lists local helplines.

## What an AI assistant can and cannot do
ECHO-AI can listen, offer general wellbeing and study ideas, and point to resources. It cannot diagnose conditions, prescribe or advise on medication, or provide crisis care. Its emotion estimates are model predictions and can be wrong.
