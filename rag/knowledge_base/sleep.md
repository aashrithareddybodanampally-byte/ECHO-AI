# Sleep Basics

## Why sleep matters
Sleep supports concentration, mood and memory. Going without enough sleep can make stress feel heavier and make studying less effective.

## Consistent schedule
Going to bed and waking up at roughly the same time each day, including weekends, helps the body keep a steady rhythm.

## Wind-down routine
A calm routine in the last 30 to 60 minutes before bed can help: dim lights, put screens away, and do something quiet such as reading or gentle stretching. Heavy meals, caffeine late in the day and intense exercise right before bed can make it harder to fall asleep.

## If you can't fall asleep
If you have been lying awake for a long time, getting up and doing something quiet in low light until you feel sleepy can help, rather than staying in bed feeling frustrated. Ongoing sleep problems are worth discussing with a doctor.
