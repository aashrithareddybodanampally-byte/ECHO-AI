# General Wellbeing

## Stress and exams
Stress before exams or deadlines is common. Breaking work into small steps, keeping regular sleep and meals, moving your body and talking to someone you trust can all make stress more manageable.

## Being kind to yourself
Harsh self-criticism tends to add to stress. Try speaking to yourself the way you would speak to a friend in the same situation, and acknowledge the effort you are making, not just the results.

## Staying connected
Spending time with friends, family or classmates, even briefly, supports wellbeing. Telling someone how you feel can make difficult periods feel less isolating.

## Basic routines
Regular meals, drinking enough water, some daily movement and time outdoors are simple routines that support energy and mood.
